MITOC Constitution Review

Why is this necessary?

Every year, MITOC's president and treasurer must re-register MITOC with MIT as a student organization. This process requires us to certify that our constitution adheres to MIT's rules. While reviewing our constitution and bylaws after the October BOD meeting, we found a number of important issues. We are therefore proposing the following changes, which are aimed at bringing MITOC into compliance with MIT's rules and fixing internal contradictions while preserving MITOC's customs and procedures to the greatest extent possible.

  1. Changes needed to follow MIT's rules:
    • Added required content: election procedure and quorum, officer terms, officer duties, and a quorum for amendments.
    • Moved quorum and voting rules into the Meetings article, where MIT requires them.
    • Added required statements to the constitution.
    • All Executive Board members (officers) must now be MIT students.
    • Constitution changes take effect once the ASA and SOLE approve them.
    • The bylaws now waive dues for MIT students who cannot afford them, as MIT requires, and require every member to sign the MIT liability waiver.
  2. Changes that fix contradictions:
    • The previous constitution contained contradictory statements about whether Members-at-Large must be students. This has been clarified.
    • Membership categories and voting rights now match.
    • Removal rules in the bylaws now follow the constitution.
    • Advisory Board appointments and special meetings are described the same way in both documents.
