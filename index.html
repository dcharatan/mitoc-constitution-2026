MITOC Constitution Review

Why is this necessary?

These changes bring MITOC's constitution in line with MIT's requirements for student group constitutions, fix some internal contradictions, and codify existing practices. The changes are as follows:

  • Add required content: election procedure and quorum, officer terms, officer duties, a quorum for amendments, and required statements.
  • Define Member-at-Large as a non-officer position. Clarify eligibility for Member-at-Large position.
  • Add an Office Access Manager, an MIT student officer who manages office access in Engage. In practice, the student Desk Captain will hold this position.
  • State that constitution changes take effect once the ASA and SOLE approve them.
  • State that dues are waived for MIT students who cannot afford them and require every member to sign the MIT liability waiver.
